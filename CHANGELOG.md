# Changelog

All notable changes to the CivicLens AI platform are documented in this file.

## [2.1.0] - 2026-11-19
### Enhanced
- **Citizen Grievance Redressal**: Real-time incident intake with optional phone number handling (no placeholder defaults displayed when optional).
- **Inspection Photo Evidence**: Integrated photographic inspection evidence viewing in Citizen Tracking and Officer Dossier inspector with full-resolution lightboxes.
- **Deep Zoom Map Integration**: Direct coordinate targeting from Administrative Dossiers with automated camera focus (`zoom=16`) and dedicated target pin on interactive Tripura GIS map.
- **Infrastructure Registry Search**: One-click asset filtering by code, category, and Tripura district from the incident inspector.
- **UX4G & GIGW 3.0 Compliance**: Centered severity pills, high-contrast Admin Command Center access banner in Government View, and accessible role switcher.
- **Platform Architecture**: Fully unified single-pane civic operations command center.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-10
### Added
- Multi-district geospatial risk analytics and computer vision damage mensuration head for Tripura public assets.
- Tamper-evident SHA-256 administrative audit trail and role-scoped permissions for Super Admin, PWD, and DWS.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-15
### Initial Release
- Core civic infrastructure management, PostGIS spatial indexing, and Next.js 15 dual-mode interface.
