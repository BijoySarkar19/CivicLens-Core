## Areas for Contribution
We actively welcome contributions in the following domains:
1. **Tripura Spatial Data**: Submitting verified GeoJSON coordinates for bridges, roads, culverts, and panchayat buildings across all 8 districts.
2. **Computer Vision Models**: Submitting annotated road surface datasets (potholes, cracks, waterlogged spans).
3. **Accessibility Testing**: Auditing WCAG 2.1 AA screen-reader navigation and UX4G compliance.
4. **Localization**: Translating citizen redressal workflows into Bengali, Kokborok, and Hindi.

Please consult [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for contribution guidelines.
