# Project Overview

CivicLens Core is a full-stack geospatial public infrastructure operations platform.

Part 1 answers:

- what infrastructure exists
- where it is
- its recorded condition
- what incidents were reported
- who reported or verified them
- what work has been assigned
- what inspections and maintenance were recorded
- what happened historically

The platform is built around a transactional PostgreSQL/PostGIS source of truth, explicit workflows, provenance metadata and auditability.

Future policy engines, notifications and AI services should extend the existing contracts rather than bypassing them.
