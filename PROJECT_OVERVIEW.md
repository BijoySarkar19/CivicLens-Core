# CivicLens AI — System Architecture & Strategic Overview

CivicLens AI is an enterprise-grade Smart Urban Governance and Public Infrastructure Intelligence platform calibrated for the State of Tripura (8 administrative districts). It unites citizen grievance redressal, geospatial infrastructure registry, predictive AI risk forecasting, computer vision defect mensuration, and departmental work order management into a single-pane operational command center.

## Core Operational Capabilities

The platform continuously monitors:
- **Infrastructure Inventory**: Exact spatial positions (EPSG:4326), operational statuses, and structural condition ratings for over 60 verified Tripura assets (bridges, arterial highways, water treatment plants, electrical substations, municipal drains).
- **Citizen Grievance Redressal**: Real-time grievance registration, phone/OTP tracking, GPS verification, and attached photographic site evidence.
- **Predictive Infrastructure Risk**: ResNet-50 computer vision damage scoring, YOLOv8 object detection, and 90-day time-series failure risk modeling.
- **Departmental Work Order Lifecycle**: Automated work ticket dispatch, field officer assignment, and milestone completion tracking for PWD and DWS directorates.
- **Auditable Administrative Control**: Role-based access control (Super Admin, PWD Engineer, DWS Inspector) backed by an immutable SHA-256 tamper-evident ledger.
