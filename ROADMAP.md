# CivicLens AI — Strategic Engineering Roadmap

## Core Operational Platform (Active)
- **Unified Command Center**: Real-time cross-departmental operations desk (Super Admin, PWD Engineer, DWS Inspector).
- **Citizen Grievance Redressal**: Multi-channel intake with optional phone numbers, OTP verification, and image evidence previews.
- **Geospatial Infrastructure Intelligence**: PostGIS EPSG:4326 spatial engine with interactive Leaflet GIS heatmap, district filters, and coordinate-focused deep zoom.
- **Predictive Asset Intelligence**: 90-day time-series failure risk predictor, ResNet-50 visual defect scoring, and YOLOv8 damage detection.
- **Auditable Administrative Control**: SHA-256 tamper-evident ledger, employee dossier inspection, and commission dismissal governance.
- **Government Compliance**: UX4G Design System, GIGW 3.0 accessibility, and dual-mode high-contrast display.

## Future Expansion & Enhancements
- Automated drone photogrammetry ingestion for rural highway culvert inspections.
- Direct SMS / WhatsApp gateway dispatch for citizen grievance status updates.
- Real-time IoT sensor telemetry streaming for municipal water reservoirs and flood embankments.
- Multi-lingual citizen support (Bengali, Kokborok, Hindi, English).
